Hearing Aids: Soundly vs AirPods Pro 3

Researched 2026-09-15, revised same day after the Sept 9, 2026 Apple event. Sister page: hearing-aids.md

Two separate decisions, not either/or. (1) Buy AirPods Pro 3 today as an FDA-cleared stop-gap (~$219–249, returnable). (2) Book the free Soundly audiogram, which tells you whether (1) is actually enough. Do both — (1) commits you to nothing and (2) is free.

The one number that decides everything

Apple's Hearing Aid feature is cleared for “perceived mild to moderate hearing loss,” adults 18+. Apple publishes no dB cutoff — “perceived” means self-assessed. The standard clinical categories:

DegreedB HL (ASHA/Clark)AirPods territory?
Mild26–40Yes — the sweet spot
Moderate41–55Upper edge of cleared range
Moderately severe56–70No — prescription aids
Severe / profound71+No
The Apple test is reported reliable up to about 55 dB — roughly where “moderate” ends anyway. Past that it's both out of clearance range and out of measurement range.

Nothing here is a guess about your hearing. Get the audiogram.

Ear tips & fit — matters more here than for music

Amplification depends on the acoustic seal. A leaky seal bleeds out amplified sound (especially low frequencies), so a bad fit reads as “the hearing aid feature doesn't work for me” when the real problem is a tip size.

Pro 3 ships five sizes: XXS, XS, S, M, L. All in the box — nothing to choose at purchase. XXS is new this generation.

  1. Run the Acoustic Seal Test first. Settings → AirPods → Acoustic Seal Test (needs iOS 26). Do this before the Hearing Test, or you calibrate your hearing profile against a bad seal and bake the error in.
  2. Expect to size down. Pro 3 tips are foam-infused silicone and deliberately firmer than Pro 2's; users consistently report needing a smaller size than before.
  3. Different size per ear is normal. Ears aren't symmetric.
Known comfort issue. Pro 3 has a real complaint trail since launch — sore ears, irritation, slipping once sweat or skin oil builds up. The firmer tip that produces the better seal is the same thing causing the soreness. Tolerable for a commute playlist; a bigger deal for all-day hearing-aid wear. Test long sessions inside your return window. If no tip size gets a comfortable all-day seal, that's a genuine argument for custom-molded prescription aids.

Recommended sequence

  1. Today — Buy AirPods Pro 3 (~$219–249). Prefer Costco for the 90-day window + AppleCare+. You want earbuds regardless.
  2. Today — Run the Acoustic Seal Test and settle tip size first, then the Hearing Test. Export the PDF audiogram.
  3. Today/tomorrow — Submit a Soundly appointment request. Free, zero obligation.
  4. Weeks 1–12 — Actually wear them: meetings, restaurants, outdoors, TV. Note where they fail. That's real data for the audiologist.
  5. At the clinic — Bring the Apple PDF. Get the proper audiogram.
  6. Decide:
    • Solidly mild and AirPods working → you may be done. Revisit later; hearing loss is progressive.
    • Moderate, or AirPods fall short in noise/all-day → prescription aids, with a 60-day refund window to prove themselves.
    • Moderately severe or worse → AirPods were never going to be enough. Nothing lost; you got earbuds and a cheap education.
The failure mode to avoid: using a good-enough stop-gap as the reason to skip the free test for two years. Book the test today too.

HSA/FSA

FDA clearance ≠ IRS eligibility. Prescription hearing aids are clearly eligible; consumer earbuds with amplification modes are generally listed as not eligible, and administrators are waiting on IRS guidance. Ask your plan administrator before assuming. Prescription aids through Soundly are normal eligible medical expenses.
