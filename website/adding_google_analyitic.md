

Set Up a Google Analytics Account:
- Go to analytics.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
Click “Start measuring” and set up an account name.
In the property setup, choose “Web” as the platform, and enter your website’s URL (e.g., http://www.yourdomain.com or the bucket’s URL like http://storage.googleapis.com/your-bucket if you haven’t set up a custom domain).
Create a data stream to get a Measurement ID (starts with G-XXXXXXX).
